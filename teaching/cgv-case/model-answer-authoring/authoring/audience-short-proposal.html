04 · Computable objective and rules

State the objective
the application actually uses.

J = R − 0.2 × U × 14 − 2 × changed showings

Penalized synthetic revenue

R: ticket plus concession revenue units.
U: people lost to capacity limits.
Changes: relative to the starting plan.

Admissions are a separately reported simulated outcome. Revenue units are neither KRW nor profit.

Explicit fixture constraints

09:00–24:30 operating window; runtime plus 20-minute cleaning; no overlap; compatible film/screen/format; capacity and protected commitments.

All values are demo assumptions. Real rights, policies and costs require confirmation.

07 · Search and later revision

Evaluate valid candidates.
Preserve the commitments.

Reference optimizer

  1. Up to 8 rounds, at most 24 mutations per round.
  2. Reject invalid schedules; rank using a 2,000-particle fast expected evaluator.
  3. Fully evaluate up to 2 finalists per round with the local population; keep the higher objective.

Missing-film coverage candidates receive finalist priority. This heuristic has no global-optimum guarantee.

VALIDATION AND EVALUATION ARE SEPARATE FROM PREDICTION ACCURACY09 / 11
09 · Validation before adoption

Test assumptions and outcomes
before making a business claim.

Data and information

Audit the target

Full supply, zero-sale screenings, capacity, versions, purchase times and check-ins.

Data owner confirms coverage and cutoff. If missing, narrow the target and defer action claims.

Models and comparisons

Held-out evidence

Common baseline, leakage-safe period, target-specific error and calibration. Compare plausible latent worlds.

A fixed seed is reproducibility, not certainty. Simulation replay is not an independent causal test.

Decision and business

Shadow, then trial

Zero hard violations; preserved bookings; agreed stability and practical-value gates.

Set thresholds before a bounded controlled pilot. Trial size and duration require an estimation plan.

Proceed if agreed gates pass. Revise unstable assumptions. Stop or narrow scope when critical data, feasibility or practical value fail.

Maximize a synthetic attendance score.

J(schedule) = round(Σ screening_attendance)
screening_attendance = min(room_capacity, response)
response = film_base × site_taste × time_factor
           × room_effect × substitution_factors × 130

What the rule represents

Time effects, capacity saturation and interactions among nearby shows.

What it does not establish

Purchased tickets, measured admissions, unique people or causal demand.

Confirm outcome unit, record semantics, exposure, schedule versions and operating rules.

Stop if critical inputs or a valid target cannot be established.

Gate 2 · Modeling

Validate response

Use time-separated data, check calibration and supported action contexts, compare with the declared baseline.

Thresholds: TBD after measuring the baseline.

Gate 3 · Operator-led trial

Measure field effects

Check independent feasibility, measured outcomes, disruptions and stability against an agreed protocol.

Revise/stop for hard-rule violations or failed trial criteria.

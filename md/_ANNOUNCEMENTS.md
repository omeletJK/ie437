## 2026-10-19 · Midterm project is out — due Mon 2 Nov, 10:00
{pin}

The midterm (30% of the grade) is a **two-week individual take-home project**, in the form of
Assignment 1: a problem definition, the code behind it, an interactive demonstration and a
proposal. A nationwide convenience-store chain must decide how many distribution centres to run
and where. You formulate the daily routing of one centre as the lower level, connect it to the
upper-level decision on the number and location of centres, search under an evaluation budget,
and say explicitly how you balanced modelling the real operation against being able to compute
an answer. The tools are those of Lectures 1, 2, 4 and 5.

Download the package in the [Midterm section](#midterm) — read the brief's *At a glance* box and
Section 5 (*How to approach it*) first. Start with a toy instance and have the whole pipeline
running by day 7.

**Released** Mon 19 Oct 10:00 · **Due** Mon 2 Nov 10:00 KST · one ZIP (≤ 200 MB) per student,
submitted on KLMS. Questions in class and by email to the TA; any answer that changes the brief
will be posted here as a new version.

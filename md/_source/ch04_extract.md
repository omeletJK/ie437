### Cut block — multi-output GPs (pp. 46–68)

Section divider p46. **ICM** (pp.47–53): two outputs from one shared latent GP sample, $f_1=a^1_1u^1$, $f_2=a^1_2u^1$; full covariance derivation ending at $\mathrm{cov}[\mathbf f(x),\mathbf f(x')]=\mathbf{aa}^\top k(x,x')=\mathbf Bk(x,x')$ with $\mathbf B$ **rank one**. pp.54–56: two latent samples ⇒ $\mathbf B$ rank two, and the stacked prior $N(0,\mathbf B\otimes\mathbf K)$. p57: Kronecker product, with coloured block pictures. pp.58–59: general case, $f_d=\sum_{i=1}^R a_d^i u^i$, $\mathbf B=\mathbf{AA}^\top$ rank $R$, $\mathbf B\otimes\mathbf K\in\R^{NR\times NR}$. **SLFM** (pp.60–65): $Q$ latent processes with *different* kernels $k_q$, $\mathbf{cov}=\sum_q \mathbf B_q k_q$, each $\mathbf B_q$ rank 1. **LMC** (pp.66–68): generalises both, $f_d=\sum_{q}\sum_{i=1}^{R_q} a^i_{d,q}u^i_q$, $\mathbf B_q$ rank $R_q$.

**Verdict: cut entirely.** Not in the tex, not needed for the thesis. At most one clause in the appendix noting that correlated outputs are handled by a coregionalisation matrix $\mathbf B$ so the joint kernel is $\mathbf B\otimes\mathbf K$.

### Cut block — tooling, constrained BO, MOBO, scalability (pp. 164–225)

- **pp.164–175 GPflowOpt.** Library built on GPflow/TensorFlow. Acquisitions supported: EI (Močkus 1975), LCB (Srinivas 2010), Max-Value Entropy Search (Wang & Jegelka 2017), PoI (Kushner 1964), Hypervolume-based PoI (Couckuyt 2014), Probability of Feasibility (Schonlau 1997). Comparison table vs GPyOpt / Spearmint / BayesOpt / RoBO. Test problems: **Branin** ($f^*=0.3978$ at three points), an 11th-degree polynomial ($f^*=1.3855$ at $x^*=-0.4121$), **Goldstein–Price** ($f^*=3$ at $(0,-1)$), Beale / Bukin N.6 / Himmelblau, airline-passenger hyperparameter tuning (Snoek 2012), modified Binh multi-objective. **Cut** — the citation years (Kushner 1964, Močkus 1975, Srinivas 2010) are worth one appendix line.
- **pp.176–185 Constrained BO** (Gardner et al., 2014). $\max_{c(x)\le\lambda} f(x)$ where $f$ and $c$ are both expensive and measured together. Constrained improvement $I_C(x)=\Delta(x)\max\{0,f(x)-f(x^+)\}$ with $\Delta(x)\in\{0,1\}$ Bernoulli, $\E[\Delta(x)]=\mathrm{PF}(x)=p(c(x)\le\lambda)$ — a univariate Gaussian CDF. Then, by conditional independence of $c$ and $f$ given $x$: $\boxed{\mathrm{EI}_C(x)=\mathrm{PF}(x)\,\mathrm{EI}(x)}$. Multiple constraints: $\mathrm{PF}=\prod_i p(c_i\le\lambda_i)$. Two flow diagrams (two GPs, one shared decision box); simulation contours comparing uniform sampling / BO / cBO. **One appendix line** — the factorisation EI × PF is elegant and cheap to state.
- **pp.186–208 Multi-objective BO.** Pareto motivation (fastest car vs most fuel-economic car). Pareto frontier / utopia point / dominance $y''\succ y'$; $P(Y)=\{y'\in Y:\{y''\in Y: y''\succ y', y''\ne y'\}=\emptyset\}$. Hypervolume $\mathrm{HV}(P)$ w.r.t. a reference $r$; $\mathrm{HVI}(P,\mathbf f)=\mathrm{HV}(P,\mathbf f)-\mathrm{HV}(P)$, zero outside the non-dominated region $A(P)$. Acquisitions: PHVI $=\int_{A(P)}p(\mathbf f)d\mathbf f$; **EHVI $=\E[\max\{0,\mathrm{HVI}\}]$ — "a generalised version of EI"**; HVPI $=\mathrm{HVI}(P,\mu(x))\times\mathrm{PHVI}$ as a cheap approximation. CMOBO = contextual + multi-objective. **Cut to one line**: EI generalises to a Pareto front by replacing "improvement over $f^{\max}$" with "hypervolume gained".
- **pp.209–225 Challenges.** (i) **Input dimension**: sample complexity exponential in $D$; the acquisition surface becomes "vast flat regions interspersed with highly non-convex landscapes"; **"in practice BO is typically limited to optimizing 10–20 parameters."** Fixes: random embeddings (Wang 2014), learned linear embeddings (Garnett 2013), **VAE-BO** in latent space (Gómez-Bombarelli, chemical design), autoencoder + response surface (Moriconi 2019). (ii) **Data points**: "typically limited to a few thousand evaluations"; $O(n^3)$; sparse/online GPs (McIntire 2016), DNGO — neural net as adaptive basis, "scales linearly with the number of data rather than cubically" (Snoek 2015), ensemble BO with Mondrian partitions (Wang 2015). (iii) **Update frequency**: skip the update when a new sample is uninformative. (iv) **GP + deep learning**: deep GPs (Damianou 2013), variational auto-encoded deep GPs (Dai 2016), deep kernel learning (Wilson 2016), **conditional neural processes** (Garnelo 2019). **One horizon slide** — "10–20 dimensions and a few thousand evaluations" is the honest limit and the reason Lecture 5 exists; VAE-BO is a direct pointer to Lecture 6.
